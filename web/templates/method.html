How it works

What a simulation does, what the gate requires, and what each strategy is. The runs themselves are on the Simulations page.

How a simulation runs

  1. PricesDaily open, high, low and close for {{ '{:,}'.format(data.names or 0) }} names since {{ data.first.strftime('%b %Y') if data.first else '—' }}, adjusted for splits and dividends. Untraded days and prices that jump 50% and snap back are removed. A session still trading is ignored until it closes.
  2. Rules, fixed in advanceEach strategy is a written rule with textbook parameters — nothing is tuned to the data.
  3. Every signal becomes a ticketA buy limit at the close, filled only if a later day trades at or below it within {{ cfg.validity_sessions }} sessions.
  4. Exits lean against the strategyStop and target in one day: the stop wins. A gap through the stop exits at the open. A rule's own exit is taken at the next open.
  5. Costs on every tradeRound trip: India {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.NSE }}%, ASX {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.ASX }}%, US {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.US }}%.
  6. Results in RProfit divided by the risk planned at entry: +1R made what it risked; −1R is a clean stop-out.
  7. Then as one accountAll passing strategies together: at most {{ cfg.max_new_ideas_per_scan }} new tickets a day, {{ cfg.max_open_positions }} positions, {{ cfg.risk_per_trade_pct }}% risk each, cash spent once — against the index, random picks and survivors.
  8. Intraday, separatelySame-day strategies run on hourly bars ({{ '{:,}'.format(hourly.n or 0) }} since {{ hourly.first.strftime('%b %Y') if hourly.first else '—' }}): entry the hour after the signal, stop at the first hour's low, out by the close. Same gate, a one-year recent period, intraday costs (India {{ cfg.intraday.round_trip_cost_pct.NSE }}%, ASX {{ cfg.intraday.round_trip_cost_pct.ASX }}%, US {{ cfg.intraday.round_trip_cost_pct.US }}%), and an account replay of at most {{ cfg.intraday.max_trades_per_day }} trades a day.
  9. Every two hoursPrices are topped up and everything re-runs. Daily bars, so a run between closes repeats the last close's verdict; each market's close reaches the evidence within two hours.

The gate

all must hold
  • At least {{ cfg.gate.min_trades_recent }} trades in the last {{ cfg.recent_period_years }} years
  • Average at least {{ '{:+.2f}R'.format(cfg.gate.min_expectancy_r) }} per trade, profit factor ≥ {{ cfg.gate.min_profit_factor }}
  • Profitable in the older period too
  • Better than the same exits on random entry days
  • 95% of 2,000 reshuffled 100-trade runs end profitable
  • The name itself: ≥ {{ cfg.gate.min_symbol_trades }} past trades, not a losing record
  • The market as one account beats its index on Sharpe and worst fall

What these numbers can't tell you

  • Survivorship
    The universe is today's index members; companies that collapsed and left since 2014 are missing, which flatters trend and momentum most. "Beat random entries" controls for part of it; the survivors line shows the rest.
  • Daily data, not intraday
    Every rule acts on closing prices. Nothing here tests trades opened and closed within a session.
  • Costs are estimates
    Pessimistic by design, but not your contract notes. Edit them in conf/advisor.yaml.
  • The real test is forward
    Every ticket sent is tracked on the Ideas page by the same rules. If live results drift below these, it shows there first.
