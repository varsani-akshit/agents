How a simulation runs

  1. PricesDaily open, high, low and close, adjusted for splits and dividends. Days a stock didn't trade, and prices that jump 50% and snap back (bad prints), are removed.
  2. Rules, fixed in advanceEach strategy is a written rule with textbook parameters — nothing is tuned to the data, so the past can't be memorised.
  3. Every signal becomes a ticketA buy limit at the close. It fills only if a later day trades at or below it, within {{ cfg.validity_sessions }} sessions.
  4. Exits lean against the strategyIf the stop and target fall in one day, the stop wins. A gap through the stop exits at the open. A rule's own exit is taken at the next open.
  5. Costs come off every tradeRound trip: India {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.NSE }}%, ASX {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.ASX }}%, US {{ cfg.round_trip_cost_pct.US }}% (brokerage, taxes, slippage, currency).
  6. Results in RProfit divided by the risk planned at entry. +1R made what it risked; −1R is a clean stop-out.
  7. Then as one accountAll passing strategies together: at most {{ cfg.max_new_ideas_per_scan }} new tickets a day, {{ cfg.max_open_positions }} positions, {{ cfg.risk_per_trade_pct }}% risk each, cash spent once.

The gate

all must hold
  • At least {{ cfg.gate.min_trades_recent }} trades in the last {{ cfg.recent_period_years }} years
  • Average at least {{ '{:+.2f}R'.format(cfg.gate.min_expectancy_r) }} per trade, profit factor ≥ {{ cfg.gate.min_profit_factor }}
  • Profitable in the older period too, not just recently
  • Better than the same exits on random entry days
  • 95% of 2,000 reshuffled 100-trade runs end profitable
  • The name itself: ≥ {{ cfg.gate.min_symbol_trades }} past trades, not a losing record
  • The market as one account beats its index on Sharpe and worst fall

What these numbers can't tell you

  • Survivorship
    The universe is today's index members. Companies that collapsed and left since 2014 are missing, which flatters trend and momentum most. "Beat random entries" controls for part of it; the survivors line shows the size of the rest.
  • Costs are estimates
    Pessimistic by design, but they are not your actual contract notes. Edit them in conf/advisor.yaml.
  • The best-names filter
    Live tickets skip names with a losing record under the strategy. That record uses all of history, so it can't be replayed honestly and the account replay leaves it out.
  • The real test is forward
    Every ticket sent is tracked on the Ideas page by the same rules. If live results drift below these, it shows there first.
